# Information, compression and auditing

Compression is assessed relative to a parameter, decision and explicit observation model. RMS, median, truncation, censoring and probability transport are different operations. Information loss does not automatically invalidate an inference: sufficiency and relevant loss depend on the problem.

For fixed positive weights and a finite Gaussian cost vector q, the law of normalized probabilities depends on contrast means and covariance: Db and DKDᵀ. Matching marginal bands is insufficient. This criterion does not assert that INPOP sampled independent marginals or establish universal GP invalidity.

Fisher, Cramér–Rao, Shannon, Rényi and decision-loss controls are conditional on their models and regularity assumptions. A retention index is not a truth probability. Synthetic results are labelled accordingly. The Cassini model retains predictive discrepancies even when numerical integration methods agree.
